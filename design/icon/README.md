# App icon: "Trio"

A rooster (centre), a duck (left) and a pigeon (right) standing together on a gold nest, on the app's deep red.

**Meaning:** different birds, one flock, one breeder. The rooster stands tallest as the heart of the farm. The duck and pigeon stand beside it, so the app serves breeders of all birds. The nest is the farm that holds them together.

| Colour | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Deep red | `#9a2d17` | Background, brand colour |
| Gold | `#d9962b` / `#e8a33d` | Rooster, nest |
| Comb red | `#d93a22` | Rooster comb and wattle |
| Green | `#2f7d4f` | Duck |
| Blue-grey | `#6f8099` | Pigeon |
| Cream | `#f6f1ea` | Outline |

Files:
- `icon.svg`: master artwork (also `public/icon.svg`)
- `icon-maskable.svg`: smaller artwork inside the Android safe zone, so round or squircle masks don't clip it
- `alt-three-feathers.svg`: alternative concept that wasn't chosen

The PNGs in `public/` (192, 512, maskable 512, apple-touch 180) are rendered from these SVGs.
